## 2. Information We Collect

### 2a. Information you provide

- Wallet addresses (Solana public keys) submitted when accepting risk disclosures,
  creating claims, or registering a username.
- Username and display tag (if using identity features).
- Support communications.

### 2b. Information collected automatically

- Transaction metadata: claim IDs, denomination, timestamps, state transitions.
- IP addresses and device fingerprints (used for security and rate-limiting).
- Compliance screening results (sanctions check outcomes).
- Audit log entries for all protocol interactions.

### 2c. On-chain data

All note state (mint, transfer, claim, redeem, burn) is permanently recorded on
the Solana blockchain and is publicly visible. We cannot delete or modify on-chain
data. Do not assume on-chain activity is private.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- **Service provision**: routing notes, resolving usernames, processing claims.
- **Security**: fraud detection, velocity analysis, anomaly detection, brute-force prevention.
- **Compliance**: sanctions screening (OFAC, EU, UN lists), AML monitoring, SAR filing.
- **Legal obligations**: responding to law-enforcement requests, regulatory reporting.
- **Analytics**: aggregate, anonymised protocol statistics.

We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties.
